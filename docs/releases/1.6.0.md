# Kestrel 1.6.0 — Software updates preview

Kestrel can now check for updates and install them with your approval.

- **Check for Updates…** is available in the wing menu, app menu, and Settings → General.
- Automatic checks are on by default. Turn them off in General settings for manual checks only. You choose when to download, install, and relaunch.
- Update feeds and downloads are signed and verified. Invalid updates are rejected, and system-profile reporting is disabled.
- The refreshed English demo covers daily tools, five themes, and update controls.

## Install or upgrade

Download `Kestrel-1.6.0-macos-arm64.zip`, unzip it, and move Kestrel.app into Applications. Version 1.5.0 and earlier need this one manual replacement to acquire the updater: quit the old app first, replace it, and reopen. Saved settings and snippets remain in place. Press **Option–Space** to start.

## Compatibility and limitations

- Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later. Build 11.
- This preview is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**. If macOS cannot verify a trusted download, follow the [first-launch guide](https://github.com/junoh-bg/kestrel/blob/main/docs/getting-started.md#first-launch). Update signing does not remove Apple's first-launch approval requirements.
- Verified locally on macOS 27.0.1, including an isolated signed upgrade and rejection of a tampered archive. A separate-Mac download and macOS 14 acceptance remain unverified.
- Ad-hoc replacements may require renewed macOS folder-access approval. Clipboard history remains optional and starts off for new installations.
